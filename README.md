## `01 / THE MINDSET`

I like building things that sit at the intersection of intelligence, engineering and real-world utility.

My interests aren't restricted to a single domain. I explore computer vision, autonomous agents, multimodal AI, financial intelligence, graph algorithms, retrieval systems and full-stack engineering.

I enjoy understanding systems from the inside out: their architecture, mathematical foundations, failure modes, performance bottlenecks and design trade-offs.

**My approach:**

- Don't just wrap an API. Understand the system behind it.
- Don't just generate answers. Build systems that can verify them.
- Don't just automate tasks. Build agents that can execute them.
- Don't just build demos. Think about reliability, security and scalability.
